Once, in a life that felt too ordinary to hold something this big, a boy met a girl who would quietly rearrange everything he thought he knew about love.

He didn't know it yet — not on that first day — but somewhere between her laugh and the way she carried her worries like they weren't allowed to weigh her down, he had already started falling.

Time passed. Miles got in the way. Distance did what distance always does — it turned small silences into big questions, and small misunderstandings into fights neither of them really wanted to have.

But through all of it, one thing never once wavered: her.

A Letter, Written Slowly

So here is his letter. Not written for a story this time, but for her — the real one, the only one:

Dear Lila,

my dearest of all, the woman that bears all my worries with me, to the woman that gives me strength when I am sorrow, to the woman that is her most feminine, to my woman, my best friend, my soulmate and my one ultimative love —

there are miles between us, and time zones that steal our mornings and our nights, and still — none of it has ever been louder than what I feel for you.

I know the distance has not been kind to us. I know it has turned small misunderstandings into arguments, and silence into worry, and made simple things — a hug, a hand to hold, a quiet evening — feel impossible when we needed them most. I am sorry for every fight that was really just distance wearing a different mask.

But I need you to know: through every mile, you have been my most constant thing. My peace. My favorite person to tell everything to, and my favorite person to tell nothing to — because with you, even silence feels safe.

— Yours, always. Zahed
